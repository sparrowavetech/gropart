# Seller Invoicing: global product plan (research update, 2026-11-03)

Status: product specification with a local 0.1.0 development foundation under `platform/plugins/seller-invoicing/`. Local plugin activation and its two-table migration have run. Minor-unit revenue reconciliation, source-drift warnings and a strict document-total validator now exist; no tax invoice, fee deduction or live deployment exists. This document is not a certification that one build complies with every jurisdiction. Tax rules, filing formats and Amazon UI can change; country packs need dated legal review before sale as compliant.

## Evidence and product lessons

- Amazon's Seller Central separates seller fee tax invoices from payment/date-range reports and merchant tax reports. Seller fee invoices can be period documents, while transaction reports explain individual charges. For India, its merchant B2B tax report is invoice-level; it is distinct from Amazon's invoice to the seller for Amazon services. See [Amazon seller fee library](https://sellercentral.amazon.com/seller-forums/discussions/t/8cd1b62a-fa15-41ee-99e7-dc13df2175fd), [Amazon India B2B merchant report](https://sellercentral.amazon.in/help/hub/reference/external/G24SPU7NGKQA2TQC?ld=ASINGeneralDirect&locale=en-IN), and [Amazon Seller Central reports overview](https://sell.amazon.in/seller-blog/introduction-to-amazon-seller-central?mons_sel_locale=en_IN). Amazon forum responses describe current UI; they are not legal authority.
- India: GST tax invoice requirements include fiscal-year unique serial number, supplier/recipient tax identity where applicable, supply description/classification, place of supply and tax split. GSTR-1 Table 14 records supplies through an ECO under section 52; merchant sales reporting is separate from operator service-fee outward supplies. See [GST invoice fields](https://einvoice6.gst.gov.in/content/e-invoice-printing-process-mandatory-fields-modes-of-irn-generation/), [GST portal Table 14 advisory](https://tutorial.gst.gov.in/downloads/news/updated_advisory_new_table1415_cr23892_sj_10.01.2024.pdf), [CBIC TCS framework](https://cbic-gst.gov.in/pdf/FAQs-TCS-30-11-2018.pdf), [IRP ECO e-invoicing](https://einvoice6.gst.gov.in/content/e-invoicing-for-e-commerce-operators/). Rate and e-invoicing thresholds must be versioned from current official notifications, not hardcoded from older FAQs.
- EU: common VAT invoice fields coexist with member-state rules, cross-border place-of-supply and possible reverse charge; one EU-wide tax toggle is insufficient. See [European Commission invoicing](https://taxation-customs.ec.europa.eu/taxation/vat/vat-businesses/invoicing_en).
- UK: marketplace deemed-supplier rules can change who owes VAT on goods, and VAT records include issued/received invoices and credit notes. See [HMRC online marketplace](https://www.gov.uk/guidance/vat-and-overseas-goods-sold-to-customers-in-the-uk-using-online-marketplaces), [HMRC records](https://www.gov.uk/charge-reclaim-record-vat/keeping-vat-records).
- US: marketplace facilitator obligations vary by state and transaction; tax on the sale must not be inferred from a fee invoice. See [Streamlined Sales Tax state guidance](https://www.streamlinedsalestax.org/for-businesses/marketplace-facilitator).
- Canada/Australia/Singapore: marketplace or platform GST responsibility and invoice requirements vary by registration and supply. See [CRA platform goods](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/businesses/topics/gst-hst-businesses/digital-economy-gsthst/charge-collect/sales-good.html), [ATO tax invoices](https://www.ato.gov.au/law/view/document?LocID=%22GST%2FGSTR20131%2FNAT%2FATO%2Ffp81%22&PiT=20231128000001), [IRAS invoicing](https://www.iras.gov.sg/taxes/goods-services-tax-%28gst%29/basics-of-gst/invoicing-price-display-and-record-keeping/invoicing-customers/index).
- Gulf is multiple regimes, not one pack: UAE full VAT invoices require TRNs, sequence, supply date, AED values and exchange-rate details where applicable; Saudi Arabia has ZATCA e-invoicing/QR and integration requirements. See [UAE Article 59](https://tax.gov.ae/Datafolder/Files/Legislation/Executive%20Regulation%20of%20Federal%20Decree%20Law%20No%208%20of%202017%20-%20Publish%2017112022.pdf), [ZATCA requirements](https://zatca.gov.sa/en/E-Invoicing/PreparingYourBusiness/Phase1/Pages/How-to-prepare.aspx).

## Existing Botble integration facts to preserve

- Marketplace supports commission, subscription and hybrid modes; global/category percentage, per-vendor fixed fee and plan override. On order completion, `OrderSupportServiceProvider::afterOrderStatusCompleted` records one `mp_customer_revenues.fee`. Its seller base excludes customer shipping, shipping tax, goods tax and payment fee. Withdrawal fee is a separate flow. No invoice should duplicate an existing deduction merely because it displays a charge.
- Marketplace has a legacy `SellerInvoiceHelper` and template, but targeted inspection found no writer for its fee/shipping/tax fields or route to its controller; `generateInvoice()` contains `dd()`. New plugin owns its own snapshots, ledger and documents. Do not modify Botble Marketplace/Ecommerce sources.
- The owner's older local `gropart-live` checkout provides a concrete reference at `admin/marketplaces/generate-seller-invoice/{revenueId}` (example revenue 679). In that **older checkout**, admin and marketplace routes lead to `SellerInvoiceController`, which downloads or streams the PDF; the revenue list links to it. Its rendered one-page PDF was freshly viewed in Chrome after the owner restored Apache. It shows issuer and seller legal identities/GSTINs, invoice/order IDs and date, place of supply, platform fee, seller commission, delivery charge, SAC codes, CGST/SGST columns, totals, signature and registered-office footer. The current checkout's dormant route/writer finding above remains distinct. Do not alter the older Botble-owned checkout while developing this plugin.
- **Measured PDF reconciliation defect:** in revenue 679, the printed platform and commission row totals are ₹28.23 and ₹7.06, summing to ₹35.29, while the printed taxable base ₹29.90 plus CGST ₹2.69 plus SGST ₹2.69 and the grand total are ₹35.28. The ₹0.01 difference is consistent with independently formatting row totals and split taxes from unrounded Twig formulas. The new engine must persist rounded line tax allocations and derive all displayed row/column/grand totals from them; assert exact equality before issue, including multi-line residual allocation. This is a local sample observation, not a finding about every old invoice.
- Do not reproduce the older implementation's arithmetic or tax resolution: it computes taxable/tax totals in Twig with floating-point expressions, presents `invoice.fee` as the grand total without a demonstrated equality check against rendered components, uses one tax rate for commission and shipping, compares raw seller and issuer state IDs to decide IGST, and reads live invoicing settings during rendering. `generateInvoice()` also has `dd($invoicePath)` after saving. The new plugin must freeze legal identities, line bases/rates/taxes/rounding and document bytes at issue; validate line sum = document total = ledger evidence; use jurisdiction rules per service line; and restrict downloads to the owning seller or authorized admin. The old checkout must remain read-only.
- `allow_vendor_manage_shipping` and `charge_shipping_per_vendor` are settings, not enough to prove each order's actual shipping responsibility. Capture selected shipping mode, parties, customer charge and seller credit at order time. In unified or mixed shipping, allocate only from evidenced quote/snapshot; unresolved amounts enter reconciliation, not an invented fee.

## Document model

1. **Buyer goods invoice**: existing Ecommerce/customer document, issued by the legally correct seller/deemed supplier. This plugin only links to it; never treats it as operator service revenue.
2. **Marketplace service tax invoice**: operator to seller for commission, fixed/closing, subscriptions or other contracted services. Per-order fee detail remains accessible on both order pages. Jurisdiction pack determines whether a legally issued invoice is per-order or consolidated by tax period; if consolidated, order page links to its fee detail and issued period invoice. Avoid duplicate tax invoices for the same taxable service.
3. **Shipping document**: customer-paid shipping retained by operator is a settlement allocation, not automatically a second seller charge. Separate operator-to-seller shipping/recovery invoice only for an agreed taxable service/reimbursement rule and correctly identified supplier. Actual courier cost to operator is an expense, not automatically seller liability.
4. **Credit/debit note**: linked to original fee invoice and refund/reversal; no silent edit to an issued document.
5. **Settlement statement**: gross goods entitlement, customer shipping allocation, service fees, tax on fees, statutory withholdings/collections, refunds/reserves, payouts, opening/closing balance. A statement is not a tax invoice.
6. **Filing/analysis exports**: operator outward-service register, seller-facing merchant sale report, statutory collection/withholding register and payment reconciliation are separate datasets. Exports state which legal entity's return they serve; no claim of direct filing until validated against a portal specification.

## Billing country/state and tax-place resolution

Botble exposes three overlapping operator-address sources with different jobs: Ecommerce General Settings is the current base-store profile; Ecommerce Store Locators are physical stores/fulfillment locations, with a primary locator that copies its name/contact/address/country/state/city/ZIP into General Settings when changed (but does not copy the VAT/tax number); Ecommerce Invoice Settings holds the billing-company override used by Ecommerce's buyer invoice helper. A primary locator or pickup warehouse is not automatically the legal service-invoice issuer. For Seller Invoicing, use the resolved Invoice Settings profile first, General Settings for missing fields, and Store Locators only as optional evidence/selection for a separately validated supplying branch. Check conflicting values in onboarding instead of silently picking one.

Ecommerce's Invoice Template settings page accepts templates through the `ecommerce_invoice_templates` filter, already used by Marketplace for subscription and payout templates. The plugin can register its seller service invoice and credit-note templates there, each with its own content, variables, customized path and preview. Template editing changes presentation of future renders only; issued PDF bytes/data remain archived and immutable. This settings-page integration is separate from the document issuer, ledger and seller/admin library.

- The operator-to-seller service invoice starts with Ecommerce **General Settings** (`store_name`, `store_country`, `store_state`, `store_city`, `store_address`, `store_zip_code`, `store_vat_number`) as the base-store profile. Ecommerce **Invoice Settings** (`company_*_for_invoicing`) take precedence where explicitly configured, matching the existing Ecommerce invoice fallback behavior. These settings seed the **supplier's legal billing entity**; they are not proof of tax registration or the correct supplying branch. Plugin onboarding shows the resolved source/value for review, validates legal entity, registration, state and address, and never silently switches source after issuance. If several registrations/branches exist, the operator selects the supplying registration under a versioned routing rule. The chosen entity is frozen on the document.
- The **recipient** comes from the seller's Marketplace tax profile (`mp_stores.tax_country`, `tax_state`, `tax_id`, and `mp_vendor_info.tax_info`) with store address/contact as display fallback. Seller profile is not the buyer's delivery address. Validate conflicting/missing seller tax fields; require an explicit billing identity and tax-registration status before issuing a tax invoice. Snapshot both IDs and human-readable names; normalize ISO country and jurisdiction codes without assuming Botble's country/state database IDs equal tax codes.
- Resolve `supplier legal establishment/registration → recipient legal tax location → service type → jurisdiction-specific place of supply → tax treatment`. Compare supplier location to **place of supply**, not blindly to the seller's store state. An invoice can have one buyer-facing shipping state, another seller tax state and a third operator registration. Shipping/courier service may need a separate rule from marketplace commission.
- India baseline for ordinary domestic B2B marketplace services: under [IGST Act section 12(2)](https://cbic-gst.gov.in/hindi/IGST-bill-e.html), place of supply for services to a registered person is generally that recipient's location, subject to service-specific exceptions. Supplier location and place of supply in the same State/UT generally leads to CGST + SGST/UTGST; different States/UTs generally leads to IGST under sections 7/8. Seller GSTIN's state prefix, selected registration and profile state must be internally consistent. Unregistered seller, SEZ, cross-border, special service and multi-registration cases go to the India pack's separate rule/review path; no default zero tax or guessed IGST.
- Example: operator registered in Maharashtra, seller registered/billed in Maharashtra → ordinary domestic B2B service normally CGST + SGST. Same operator, seller registered/billed in Karnataka → normally IGST. A Karnataka delivery address for the buyer does not change the seller-service invoice's recipient location. These examples do not decide goods invoice tax.
- International packs decide place of supply and reverse charge/deemed supplier rules individually. For example, [EU B2B service place of taxation](https://taxation-customs.ec.europa.eu/taxation/vat/vat-directive/place-taxation_en) is generally where the business customer is established, with exceptions. Country/state comparison alone cannot calculate US sales tax, EU VAT, Gulf VAT or withholding.
- Settings/profile edits affect future documents only. Drafts revalidate before issuance; issued documents retain snapshots. Missing or conflicting country/state/registration blocks tax invoice issuance and enters a review queue; a commercial statement may still show evidenced amounts without tax claims.

## Calculation and liability invariants

- Money uses currency minor units and explicit decimal/rate rounding; currency precision is configurable (zero/three-decimal currencies included). Store original transaction currency, tax currency and exchange rate/source/date when required. No float-based invoice arithmetic.
- For each vendor sub-order: `customer_collected = merchandise + goods_tax + customer_shipping + shipping_tax + payment_fee - customer_discounts` according to the immutable order snapshot; reconcile to actual receipts separately. `seller_payable = seller_goods_entitlement + seller_shipping_credit - contracted_service_charges - tax_on_service_charges - statutory_withholdings - refunds/reserves + credits`. A charge appears once in the seller ledger; a displayed retained shipping allocation does not produce a second debit.
- Never infer paid from order completion, failed/pending gateway attempt, or courier delivery. COD advance, courier collection and remittance use verified settlement evidence. Fee issuance timing is configurable only within a jurisdiction's approved tax point and must handle unpaid/cancelled orders.
- Separate tax on buyer goods from tax on operator services. GST TCS/income-tax TDS or foreign equivalents are pass-through liabilities, not operator income. Refunds create referenced adjustments; already filed periods remain historically stable.
- Snapshots freeze legal names, addresses, registration numbers, place of supply, fee contract version, tax rule version, goods/shipping allocation, currency and order reference. Corrections issue notes with audit trails.

## Cancellation, failed delivery and return lifecycle

Research: Amazon India distinguishes buyer-requested cancellation from seller-caused cancellation in its [India fee schedule](https://sellercentral.amazon.in/gp/help/external/G200336920?locale=en-IN); Amazon US may reverse referral fees while retaining a documented refund administration fee under its [US refund policy](https://sellercentral.amazon.com/help/hub/reference/external/G200387220?locale=en-GB). These are examples of explicit commercial policies, not rates to copy. For issued tax invoices, India [CGST section 34](https://cbic-gst.gov.in/hindi/CGST-bill-e.html) and the [GST portal's credit/debit-note workflow](https://tutorial.gst.gov.in/userguide/returns/Creation_of_Outward_Supplies_Return_in_GSTR-1.htm) require linked correction records; [HMRC guidance](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/help-with-vat-compliance-controls-guidelines-for-compliance-gfc8/order-to-cash-part-3--3) likewise describes credit notes for cancellation/returns. The exact tax point, eligibility and deadlines belong to the jurisdiction pack.

| Event | Buyer money and goods | Seller fee and shipping treatment | Documents |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Customer cancels before shipment | Track actual capture/refund; no sale recognized merely from placed order | Reversible commission/closing fees become zero unless contract explicitly retains an incurred service charge. Unbooked courier cost zero; booked label/cancellation charge requires carrier evidence and contract rule. Customer shipping refunded/retained only according to actual refund and policy; never double-debit seller. | If fee tax invoice unissued, cancel draft; if issued, linked credit note. Buyer invoice correction belongs to legal goods supplier. |
| Seller cancels before shipment or fails dispatch SLA | Same buyer refund evidence; seller-at-fault reason must be recorded and appealable | Configured seller cancellation penalty may apply only if published contract allows it; show distinct fee/tax line. Reversal of original sale commission is separate from penalty. Carrier cancellation charge, if any, needs invoice/label evidence. | Fee credit note for reversed charges plus separate invoice/debit note for valid penalty under local tax rules. |
| Carrier fails pickup or platform cancels | Buyer refund/reschedule based on actual state | No automatic seller penalty. Operator/carrier liability and recoverable claim tracked separately; release any provisional seller charge after adjudication. | Correct issued charges through linked notes, preserve original audit trail. |
| In transit delivery failure / RTO / refused delivery | `failed delivery` is not a confirmed return or refund. Wait for carrier event, physical RTO receipt, COD remittance/shortage and buyer refund decision | Forward/RTO courier invoices, insurance/claims and liability may differ. Attribute expense to operator, seller, buyer or carrier only by contract + evidenced reason. Original commission reversal or retention is policy-specific; no auto charge from a status label. | Hold disputed items in reconciliation; issue fee credit/debit notes after amount and liability are known. |
| Delivered then partial/full return or refund | Item/quantity-level refund and tax correction; verify gateway and COD evidence | Reverse corresponding commission proportionally from frozen original fee base, subject to contracted retained fees. Recompute only affected item allocations; shipping/refund handling follows actual amount refunded and agreed liability. Never reverse fixed fee twice. | Partial/full credit note linked to issued service invoice, plus separate buyer goods credit note by lawful issuer. |
| Replacement / redelivery | Link to original return and new shipment; no duplicate revenue | New fee only if contract makes replacement a new chargeable service; new shipping cost requires evidence. | Cross-reference original and replacement documents. |

Lifecycle states are separate axes: order, payment/capture/refund, fulfillment/carrier, physical return, settlement/remittance, fee assessment, tax document issuance and dispute. A webhook status alone does not finalize liability. Persist append-only events with source, timestamp, actor, reason, carrier evidence, payment reference and idempotency key. Use deterministic event transitions and a human review queue for missing evidence or contradictory events. Cancellation reason codes include buyer-requested, seller-fault, platform-fault, carrier-fault, force-majeure and unknown; `unknown` blocks penalties. Preserve chronology when late webhooks arrive after refund or invoice issue.

Fee policy versions must explicitly specify assessment trigger, reversal trigger, retention/penalty cap, who bears forward/RTO/cancellation shipping, how customer shipping refunds are allocated, evidence requirements, dispute window and tax treatment. Default policy is conservative: no seller penalty or unreconciled shipping recovery. The plugin records a separate carrier payable/claim and seller liability; carrier expense is not automatically marketplace revenue.

## Sale-ready architecture

- New `seller-invoicing` plugin depends only on Ecommerce and Marketplace. No Gropart, Farmart, ShipMozo, Advanced COD or Indian GST class dependency. Optional adapters consume public hooks/contracts and gracefully degrade when another plugin is absent. Theme-neutral admin routes and vendor dashboard views; scoped view composer/extension only where a supported hook is absent, tested per Botble version. Never patch parent/theme/vendor packages.
- Core contracts: `OrderSnapshotProvider`, `FeePolicyResolver`, `ShippingResponsibilityResolver`, `TaxJurisdictionResolver`, `TaxDocumentIssuer`, `FilingExporter`, `SettlementReconciler`. Other plugins may register providers with priorities and source evidence. Unknown tax/responsibility blocks tax invoice issuance and gives a clear reconciliation status.
- Tables owned by plugin: policy versions, order financial snapshots, itemized fee/settlement ledger, invoice/line/tax snapshots, credit/debit notes, number sequences scoped to legal entity/jurisdiction/fiscal year, export jobs, audit events and attachment hashes. Foreign IDs reference Ecommerce/Marketplace but no upstream schema alteration. Use unique event/idempotency keys and transactions/locks.
- UI: order detail near existing buyer invoice controls, seller and admin document library, filtering by seller/order/date/type/status/jurisdiction, role-scoped PDF/CSV/ZIP, accounting reconciliation dashboard, missing-tax-data queue, and export history. Seller never accesses another seller's document. Admin permissions split view/issue/void/export/configure; issuance is a controlled action, downloads read-only.
- Translation: all labels/errors/email strings in package locales; locale fallback, RTL-safe rendering, Unicode fonts, language-independent amounts/data, ISO country/currency codes and timezone-aware dates. Currency and tax labels generated from jurisdiction pack, not hardcoded `GST` globally.
- Country packs: India first; EU packs by member state, UK, US by state, UAE, Saudi, Canada by province, Australia, Singapore in separately reviewed releases. Generic unregistered/unknown jurisdiction mode offers commercial statements only, with tax invoice issuance disabled until a pack validates configuration. E-invoice/IRN/ZATCA/Peppol integrations are optional certified connectors; PDF alone does not fulfill mandated electronic exchange.

## Further product scope for a distributable plugin

- **Capability detection and version range:** at install/upgrade, check Ecommerce/Marketplace activation, supported Botble API/hooks and database columns. Register integration adapters by observed capability, not theme name. The library must work as a plugin-owned page even if a theme offers no adjacent order button; show an explicit compatibility result for optional buttons. Maintain a tested version matrix and migration fixtures for upgrade from older plugin releases. Botble's [plugin structure](https://docs.botble.com/cms/plugin-structure.html) supports self-contained providers, routes, permissions and migrations.
- **Tax-document lifecycle:** distinguish `draft`, `ready`, `issued`, `externally_registered`, `credited`, `voided_where_legally_allowed`, and `filing_exported`. A PDF preview is not an issued invoice. For India e-invoicing where applicable, IRN cancellation can be time limited; after that, correction may require a credit/debit note ([IRP cancellation guidance](https://einvoice6.gst.gov.in/content/kb/cancelling-e-invoice/)). Store external IDs/acknowledgements and fail safely if registration is unavailable. Never present an unregistered PDF as a valid e-invoice.
- **Accounting and reconciliation:** export general-ledger journal batches with account mappings for commission revenue, shipping revenue/expense, tax payable, statutory collections/withholdings, seller payable, refunds, reserves and write-offs. Match gateway receipts, COD remittance, carrier bills and bank payouts to order-level evidence. Detect negative seller balances and unsettled disputes; do not silently net one seller's liability against another's funds.
- **Fee transparency and seller disputes:** versioned published fee schedule with effective date, seller acceptance where required, per-order calculation explanation, invoice dispute/correction workflow, optional manual adjustment requiring reason and separate permission, and immutable actor history. Optional rules for cancellation or RTO charges default off until configured and contract-backed.
- **Operational security:** tenant-scoped signed/private downloads, no predictable public PDF URL, rate limits on bulk exports, audit logs for issue/download/void/export, data minimization in logs, retention controls per jurisdiction and safe deactivation/uninstall that preserves issued financial documents. Separate permission for seeing tax IDs from merely seeing order details.
- **Change monitoring:** country packs record legal source, effective date, reviewer and last verified date; old invoices keep the rule version used at issue. EU's [ViDA timeline](https://taxation-customs.ec.europa.eu/taxation/vat/vat-digital-age-vida_en) illustrates why future e-invoicing/reporting requirements need versioned adapters rather than a permanent global rule.

## Open product choices before implementation

- Which Botble version range is initially supported, and whether seller fee tax invoice is per order or consolidated by period for each jurisdiction. Per-order detail is always available; issuing both would duplicate a taxable document.
- Whether marketplace itself is merchant of record for any category/country; who supplies shipping to buyer/seller; whether contract authorizes any shipping cost recovery beyond customer shipping retention.
- Which countries are in the first legally reviewed release. Architecture supports additional packs, but tax compliance cannot be truthfully promised for unreviewed countries.
